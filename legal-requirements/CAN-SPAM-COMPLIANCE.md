# CAN-SPAM Act Compliance Requirements for AICalledIT

## Overview
As an email newsletter service, AICalledIT must comply with the CAN-SPAM Act of 2003. This document outlines required elements for every commercial email sent.

## Required Email Elements

### 1. Header Information
- **From Line**: Must clearly identify AICalledIT as the sender
- **Subject Line**: Must be truthful and accurately describe email content
- **Reply-To**: Must be a monitored email address

### 2. Physical Address
Every email must include our physical business address:
```
AICalledIT
[Your Los Angeles, CA Business Address]
Los Angeles, CA, USA
```

### 3. Clear Unsubscribe Mechanism
- Prominent unsubscribe link in every email
- Must process unsubscribe requests within 10 business days
- Cannot require login or payment to unsubscribe
- Cannot sell or transfer email addresses of people who unsubscribe

### 4. Commercial Email Identification
Include clear identification that email contains commercial content related to sports betting analysis.

### 5. Truthful Content
- All claims about betting analysis must be truthful
- Performance statistics must be accurate and verifiable
- No deceptive subject lines or content

## Compliance Checklist for Each Email
- [ ] "From" field identifies AICalledIT
- [ ] Subject line is truthful and descriptive
- [ ] Physical address included in footer
- [ ] Unsubscribe link prominently displayed
- [ ] Commercial nature clearly identified
- [ ] All content is truthful and accurate
- [ ] Reply-to address is monitored

## Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Up to $50,120 per non-compliant email
- FTC enforcement actions
- State attorney general actions
- Potential criminal charges for egregious violations

## Best Practices
1. Monitor unsubscribe requests daily
2. Keep detailed records of all email sends
3. Maintain clean email lists
4. Honor all unsubscribe requests promptly
5. Use double opt-in for new subscriptions
6. Regularly audit email templates for compliance

## Implementation Notes
- Update email service provider (Resend) configuration
- Test unsubscribe functionality regularly
- Train staff on CAN-SPAM requirements
- Regular compliance audits
